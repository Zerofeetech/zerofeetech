TRANSPARENCY

What “free-first” means

No pretend AI claims. No hidden usage promises.

These included utilities run in your browser and do not require a paid AI API. The writing and prompt builders use templates; they are not a ChatGPT replacement. Image editing currently covers basic adjustments only—not generative editing. Advanced AI chat, image generation, background removal, speech, and large file processing need suitable model/API integrations or additional services, which may have free-tier limits and can change over time.

Never paste passwords, payment information or confidential customer data into tools unless you understand where it is processed. Sign-up and saved workspace features depend on the server/database configuration.
